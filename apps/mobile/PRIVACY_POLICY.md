# Physical Capability Cloud — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [DATE]
**Provider:** [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("we", "us"), operator of Physical Capability
Cloud ("PCC") at https://capability.network.

This policy explains what the PCC mobile app and service collect, why, and your
choices. The app is a companion to the PCC web service; the same terms apply to
both.

## What we collect

- **Account & contact** — the email address or wallet address you use to create
  an API key/account, and a display name.
- **Authentication** — passkey **credential IDs and public keys** used to sign in
  and approve actions. Private keys are generated and held on your device's secure
  hardware and are **never** transmitted to us.
- **Transaction & settlement records** — jobs you create or fulfil, escrow and
  settlement history, and fiat on/off-ramp activity. On-chain settlement records
  are written to a public blockchain (Base) and are public and immutable by design.
- **Operator evidence** — if you capture job evidence, the photos and device/sensor
  readings you submit (camera access is used only when you take evidence).
- **Approximate location** — if you register a physical site (kernel), the location
  you provide for it.
- **Device & diagnostics** — app/usage analytics and crash diagnostics, collected
  via our processors (e.g. product analytics and error reporting) to keep the
  service working and secure.

## How we use it

To provide and operate the service; authenticate you and authorize approvals;
create, price, and settle jobs; verify evidence and compliance; process payments
through our ramp partners; and diagnose crashes and improve reliability.

## How it's shared

- **Service providers / processors** — analytics, error reporting, and payment
  partners (card/bank/mobile-money ramps) that process data on our behalf.
- **Blockchain** — settlement records are published on a public blockchain and are
  visible to anyone.
- We do **not** sell your personal information.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). API keys are stored in your device's
secure storage; passkey private keys stay in device secure hardware.
